2. COMPONENT-SPECIFIC CALCULATION LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------
2.1 BEAM PLASTIC HINGES (WFBW, CPC, RBS):
Backbone formulas are derived from empirical regressions and experimental databases
governed by TEASPA V5.0 (NCREE-25-002) and Fathisepahvand et al. (2026). The user
is strictly responsible for verifying that member depth, flange slenderness (bf/2tf),
web slenderness (h/tw), and unbraced length (Lb/ry) satisfy the published
applicability bounds and detailing provisions.

2.2 SQUARE CFST COLUMNS (CONCRETE-FILLED STEEL TUBES):
The CFST flexural-axial plastic hinge formulations are based on Lin, Huang,
Fathisepahvand et al. (2026) in the Journal of the Chinese Institute of Engineers
(JCIE). Applicability is calibrated for square steel tubes with specific width-to-
thickness (B/t) limits, concrete compressive strengths (fc'), and axial load
ratios (P/P0). Users must independently verify confinement, shear capacity, and
weld detailing against governing codes (e.g., AISC 360, ACI 318, Eurocode 4).

2.3 HOLLOW STRUCTURAL SECTION (HSS) / BOX COLUMNS:
Axial force levels directly influence flexural hinge yield scale, plastic rotation,
and degradation. Users must verify whether the direct axial load (P) or axial
force ratio (P/Pye) corresponds to the critical gravity-plus-lateral load
combination prescribed by the governing building code.

2.4 CONVENTIONAL STEEL BRACES:
H-shaped brace axial hinges are based on localized AISC-style buckling formulations.
The user must review effective length factor (K), unbraced length (Lb), and
radius of gyration (r). Slenderness limits (KL/r), gusset plate stiffness, and
fracture life under cyclic reversal must be independently checked.

2.5 BUCKLING-RESTRAINED BRACES (BRB):
The BRB backbone provided by this software is an engineering-assistance
nonlinear-static core representation based on ASCE/SEI 41-23 Chapter 9 and
ANSI/AISC 342-22.
  - The software DOES NOT verify BRB casing stability, unrestrained end connections,
    gusset plate buckling, beam-column joint stiffness, or out-of-plane stability.
  - The software DOES NOT produce a calibrated cyclic hysteretic model for
    nonlinear response-history analysis.
  - Where required by building codes or project specifications, manufacturer
    qualification-test data for the specific proprietary core and casing system
    SHALL GOVERN over all software-generated preliminary estimates.

TEASPA-PHD reads and patches text model files (.e2k for ETABS, .s2k/.txt for

3.2 COMPATIBILITY RISKS:

engineering values before running pushover or dynamic analyses.
